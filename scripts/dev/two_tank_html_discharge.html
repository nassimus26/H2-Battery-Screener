Negative side: N-H2 -> N + 2H+ + 2e-  ·  Positive side: P + 2H+ + 2e- -> P-H2
In the tanks, the bright layer is charged liquid (N-H2 on the left, P on the right) and the dark part is spent liquid; discharging empties the bright layers.
The hydrogen never becomes a gas: it travels as protons and electrons from one molecule to the other. Energy is set by the tanks (litres of liquid); power by the cell area (m2). Tanks, pumps and pipes are priced from a built 10 kW flow battery (Noack et al. 2016), scaled to the system size.
