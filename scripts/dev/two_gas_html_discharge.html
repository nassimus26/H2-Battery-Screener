Discharging. fuel gas oxidant gas water H+ through the membrane e- through the wire
Negative side: 2 H2 -> 4H+ + 4e-  ·  Positive side: O2 + 4H+ + 4e- -> 2 H2O
Drawn for hydrogen + oxygen. Other cells work the same way: the fuel (methane, acetylene...) gives up its hydrogen as protons and electrons and its product (CO2...) goes to its own tank; the oxidant (O2, Cl2...) takes them up. Nothing leaves the battery: every gas and the water are kept, so the same cell can be charged again (it works as a fuel cell). Energy is set by the tanks; power by the cell area.
